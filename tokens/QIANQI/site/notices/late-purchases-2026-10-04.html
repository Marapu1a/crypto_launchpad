RULE UPDATE · 4 OCTOBER 2026

A later check.
A future chance.

Some purchase routes need more checks. If an earlier purchase is verified as eligible, its tickets will become available for future draws. Draws already locked or completed stay unchanged.

This update takes effect no earlier than 5 October 2026, 11:30 UTC (14:30 Moscow time), and only after the confirmation system is ready. Purchases made during the notice period can still qualify after verification. Waiting does not erase a purchase.

Token trading is live. The prize draws and late-confirmation processing are not live yet. This announcement does not switch them on.

What counts

Every 100 USDG in qualifying purchases adds one Short ticket and one Monthly ticket. Smaller amounts carry forward. A confirmed purchase is counted once. Its original transaction and later confirmation remain recorded.

This update covers the checked Pons router routes into the QIANQI curve: USDG directly, or ETH converted through WETH into USDG. The buyer must receive the QIANQI in the same wallet. Other routes remain under review; using the Pons interface alone does not guarantee eligibility.

For these USDG purchases, we count the buyer’s verified net USDG debit, including the route fee and subtracting refunds. For these ETH purchases, we count the USDG delivered to the curve. Gas and native route fees do not count toward tickets.

Holding, transfers and sales do not earn tickets. No win or return is guaranteed.
