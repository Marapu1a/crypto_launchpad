The same ticket rules.

We have checked additional ETH purchase routes into the QIANQI curve. Eligible earlier purchases through these routes can receive tickets for future draws once their verification is confirmed onchain.

The notice period starts on 5 October 2026 at 15:10 UTC. Confirmation under this update may begin no earlier than 6 October 2026 at 15:10 UTC, after the required checks pass.

Existing draws and payments continue during this period. Already locked or completed draws will not change.

What counts

Every 100 USDG in qualifying purchases adds one Short ticket and one Monthly ticket. Smaller purchases add up. For these ETH routes, the amount counted is the USDG paid into the QIANQI curve. Gas, native route fees and internal vault transfers do not count. Purchases are counted once; holding, transfers and sales do not earn tickets.

The payer must receive the QIANQI in the same wallet. The checked aggregation route can leave a rounding remainder of one smallest QIANQI unit (one wei) with its intermediary. Unreviewed commands, changed contract code or a different recipient remain unsupported.
