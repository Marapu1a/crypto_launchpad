A FEW REASONABLE QUESTIONS

Can I buy IGW yet?

Not yet. There's no published IGW contract or official trading link. Both will appear here after launch.

Is there a guaranteed winner every day?

No. The proposed draw interval is 24 hours, but each draw also depends on funding and participation conditions. If those conditions aren't met, the draw waits.

Does holding IGW earn tickets?

No. The planned system awards tickets based on eligible purchases, not simply holding tokens. Final eligibility and ticket processing rules will be published before launch.

What does “24 hours” mean?

The proposed interval starts after the previous completed draw. It doesn't guarantee a payout every 24 hours.
