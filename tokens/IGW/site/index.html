A FEW REASONABLE QUESTIONS

Can I buy IGW yet?

Not through this site. We haven't published a verified IGW contract or trading link. Those will be added here when the launch is complete.

Is there a guaranteed winner every day?

No. A draw is subject to its funding, timing and participation rules. A prize is not guaranteed. The final rules will be available before launch.

Does holding the token earn tickets?

The planned model uses eligible purchase volume, not simply your wallet balance. Purchase verification can take time; final eligibility and late-ticket rules will be published at launch.

What does “24 hours” mean?

The proposed interval is measured from the last completed draw. If the conditions aren't met, the draw waits. It is not a promise of a payout at a fixed time each day.
