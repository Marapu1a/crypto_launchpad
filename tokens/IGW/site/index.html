A FEW REASONABLE QUESTIONS

Where do I buy IGW?

Use the Pons link in Token & Links and check the contract address. Never rely on a ticker alone.

Is there a guaranteed winner every day?

No. The draw interval is 24 hours, but each draw also depends on funding and participation conditions. If those conditions aren't met, the draw waits.

Does holding IGW earn tickets?

No. Tickets come from eligible purchases, not simply holding tokens. Small purchases add up. Purchases awaiting verification stay pending; once confirmed, their tickets enter future draws.

What does “24 hours” mean?

The interval starts after the previous completed draw. It doesn't guarantee a payout every 24 hours.
